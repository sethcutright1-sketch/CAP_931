## Company Strategy
- Best Buy's stated purpose is "to enrich lives through technology," combining tech expertise with a human touch to meet everyday customer needs online, in stores, or in customers' homes. (Source: https://corporate.bestbuy.com/about-best-buy/)
- Best Buy is accelerating four priorities: advance as a retail, media & advertising and technology company; expand and grow reach; elevate the customer experience; and be a human-powered, customer-focused company. (Source: https://corporate.bestbuy.com/executive-team/)

### Competitor products (from their own pages)
- Google Home app: redesigned to make it easier to control smart home devices from anywhere. (Source: https://store.google.com/)
- Pixel Tablet and Nest Hub Max: displays for keeping an eye on the front door, adjusting home temperature, and getting hands-free help from Google Assistant. (Source: https://store.google.com/)
- Nest Learning Thermostat (4th gen) and Nest Thermostat: keep the home comfortable and help save on energy. (Source: https://store.google.com/)
- Samsung aims to help users create an AI-powered home that makes life more comfortable and looks after loved ones. (Source: https://www.samsung.com/us/smart-home/)
- SmartThings is positioned to save time and energy and to expand the AI home, and is available on Samsung.com. (Source: https://www.samsung.com/us/smart-home/)
- The SmartThings app connects, controls, and automates the smart home from one app, with compatibility across hundreds of devices and brands. (Source: https://www.samsung.com/us/smart-home/)
- AI Energy Mode helps control home energy use by optimizing devices to save power and lower bills. (Source: https://www.samsung.com/us/smart-home/)
- Devices can be added in SmartThings through an auto pop-up, the "+" button, or the SmartThings QR code sticker. (Source: https://www.samsung.com/us/smart-home/)
- "AI Living" is an AI Home powered by SmartThings and Galaxy AI, and SmartThings for Appliances is listed in the site navigation. (Source: https://www.samsung.com/us/smart-home/)
