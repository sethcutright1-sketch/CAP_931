## Company Strategy
- Best Buy is evolving its Executive Team as part of a CEO transition to accelerate four priorities: (1) advance as a retail, media & advertising and technology company, (2) expand and grow its reach, (3) elevate the customer experience, and (4) be a human-powered, customer-focused company. (Source: https://corporate.bestbuy.com/executive-team/)
- Best Buy's stated purpose is to "enrich lives through technology." It delivers this through advice, products and services in stores, online and in homes, combining tech expertise and human touch to meet customers' everyday needs. (Source: https://corporate.bestbuy.com/executive-team/, https://corporate.bestbuy.com/about-best-buy/)
- Frank Bedo's new role covers growth opportunities, investments, new partnerships and customer experiences. (Source: https://corporate.bestbuy.com/executive-team/)

### Competitor products (from their own pages)
- Google positions its Pixel ecosystem as "engineered to pair perfectly with your Android phone," with Google AI (Gemini) powering Pixel features. (Source: https://store.google.com/)
- Google's smart home push centers on making "your home more helpful with Matter," connecting thousands of smart home products. (Source: https://store.google.com/)
- Google's smart home offering includes a redesigned Google Home app for controlling devices remotely and Google Assistant. Its Nest devices are Nest Hub (2nd gen), Nest Hub Max, Nest Cam, Nest Doorbell, Nest Learning Thermostat (4th gen) and Google Home Speaker. (Source: https://store.google.com/)
- Pixel Tablet on its Charging Speaker Dock lets users watch the front door, adjust the home's temperature, and ask Google Assistant for hands-free help. (Source: https://store.google.com/)
- Google offers a Google Home Premium subscription, a Nest Security Package, and a home consultation with partner OnTech Smart Services offering personalized advice. (Source: https://store.google.com/)
- The Google Store page shows no budgeting, meal planning, pantry tracking, grocery ordering, or kids' chore features. (Source: https://store.google.com/)
- Samsung positions SmartThings as the hub of an "AI-powered home" that makes life more comfortable and "looks after your loved ones." It ties this to "AI Living," combining SmartThings and Galaxy AI. (Source: https://www.samsung.com/us/smart-home/)
- Samsung's pitch is "One app. Many brands," compatible with "hundreds of devices and brands" and sold directly on Samsung.com. Its value is framed around four themes: Care (for loved ones), Secure (everyday life), Save (time and energy), and Play. (Source: https://www.samsung.com/us/smart-home/)
- SmartThings lets users connect, control and automate a smart home from one app. AI Energy Mode optimizes devices to save power and lower bills. (Source: https://www.samsung.com/us/smart-home/)
- Devices can be added through an auto pop-up in the app, the "+" button, or the SmartThings QR code sticker on the product. Related products on the page include Bespoke AI appliances, Family Hub™+ refrigerators, Galaxy devices, SmartTag, "SmartThings for Appliances," and Samsung Rewards. (Source: https://www.samsung.com/us/smart-home/)

## Leadership
- Jason Bonfig is the incoming chief executive officer. Corie Barry, the current CEO, departs Oct. 31. (Source: https://corporate.bestbuy.com/executive-team/)
- Frank Bedo is chief strategy and growth officer, reporting to Bonfig. (Source: https://corporate.bestbuy.com/executive-team/)
- Luke Motschenbacher is chief retail and services officer, reporting to Bonfig. (Source: https://corporate.bestbuy.com/executive-team/)
- Neal Sample is chief digital and technology officer. (Source: https://corporate.bestbuy.com/executive-team/)
- Jennie Weber is chief marketing officer. (Source: https://corporate.bestbuy.com/executive-team/)
- Patrick McGinnis is chief revenue officer, Lisa Valentino is chief ads and media officer, and Duane Scarboro is chief fulfillment and operations officer. All three report to Bonfig. (Source: https://corporate.bestbuy.com/executive-team/)
- Matt Bilunas, CFO, is stepping down July 31, and an external CFO search is underway. (Source: https://corporate.bestbuy.com/executive-team/)
