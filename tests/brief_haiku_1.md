## Company Strategy
- Best Buy lists four strategic priorities: advance as a retail, media & advertising and technology company; expand and grow reach; elevate the customer experience; and be a human-powered, customer-focused company. (Source: https://corporate.bestbuy.com/executive-team/)
- Leadership changes are tied to a CEO transition, with new executives appointed to accelerate these priorities. (Source: https://corporate.bestbuy.com/executive-team/)
- Best Buy's stated purpose is to "enrich lives through technology," combining tech expertise with a human touch to meet everyday customer needs online, in stores, or in their homes. (Source: https://corporate.bestbuy.com/about-best-buy/)

### Competitor products (from their own pages)
- Google positions its Pixel "ecosystem engineered to pair perfectly with your Android phone." (Source: https://store.google.com/)
- Google controls its smart home products (Nest cameras, doorbells, thermostats, displays, and Yale Smart Lock with Matter) through the redesigned Google Home app, including remotely. (Source: https://store.google.com/)
- Samsung positions SmartThings as a way to create an AI-powered home that makes life more comfortable and "looks after your loved ones," with a focus on care, security, saving time and energy, and play. (Source: https://www.samsung.com/us/smart-home/)
- Samsung promotes an "AI Living" experience powered by SmartThings and Galaxy AI, with the stated goal of simplifying life and expanding the AI home. (Source: https://www.samsung.com/us/smart-home/)
- The SmartThings app lets users connect, control, and automate a smart home from one app, with compatibility across hundreds of devices and brands. (Source: https://www.samsung.com/us/smart-home/)
- SmartThings AI Energy Mode optimizes devices to save power and lower energy bills. (Source: https://www.samsung.com/us/smart-home/)
- SmartThings devices are added through an auto pop-up on the app, the "+" button, or by scanning the SmartThings QR code on the product. (Source: https://www.samsung.com/us/smart-home/)
- Samsung's related offerings include Galaxy AI, Bespoke AI appliances, and SmartThings for Appliances. (Source: https://www.samsung.com/us/smart-home/)
